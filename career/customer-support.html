Case studies

De-escalating a chargeback threat and retaining the customer

What I faced
An unhappy subscriber was threatening to dispute his payment and cancel. He had made a specific request, and the team’s previous replies had left his main concern unacknowledged.
What I did
I read the conversation from the beginning, compared his request with the team’s replies, and named the specific concern we had missed. I acknowledged our part in the misunderstanding before discussing what would meet his needs.
What changed
The customer calmed down, kept his subscription and upgraded to a larger package.

Resolving a paid request left open by a previous agency

What I faced
On a newly transitioned account, a subscriber was hesitant to make another purchase. I found a paid custom order in the conversation history that had gone months without an update from the previous agency.
What I did
I took ownership of the outstanding request, retrieved the original details, and worked directly with the subscriber to agree on a new custom order and close out the remaining payment.
What changed
The subscriber completed the payment for the new order, and the outstanding issue was resolved.

Helping children reconnect during COVID isolation

What I faced
At Jolly Kids Preschool, parents told us their children were struggling with isolation and missing contact with classmates.
What I did
I started weekly individual check-ins for children who needed extra support and brought the class together so they could talk about their week and reconnect.
What changed
Parents said the check-ins helped their children regain a sense of normality.
