Case studies

Keeping track of concurrent conversations

What I faced
Several customers needed replies or follow-up at the same time. When someone returned or a teammate took over, they needed enough context to pick up the thread.
What I did
I clarified what each customer needed, handled objections in writing and kept notes on the conversation and the next step.
What changed
Teammates had context for the handover, and I could follow up when a customer returned.

Helping customers renew

What I faced
Renewals came up in conversations where customers had different questions or reasons for hesitating.
What I did

What changed
I helped more than 30 customers resubscribe and switch on auto-renewal.
