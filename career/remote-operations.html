Case studies

Coordinating work across six client brands

What I faced
At Worded Brands, I needed to keep track of campaign work for several brands alongside my manager’s appointments and commitments.
What I did
I supported content calendars and audience research, wrote campaign copy, tracked campaign performance and prepared summaries, sent reminders, took meeting notes and maintained financial records in Google Sheets.
What changed
I supported six client brands and gave the manager a record of commitments and campaign activity to refer back to.

Bringing field documents into one case tracker

What I faced
A legal practice needed a way for field agents to submit documents over WhatsApp and for office staff to see which cases needed attention.
What I did
I designed a CRM and case-tracking dashboard that linked WhatsApp document intake with central case records.
What changed
Five field agents could submit documents through WhatsApp, and three office staff could monitor cases from shared records.

Capturing enquiries and following up on payments

What I faced
A field-services business needed to capture incoming enquiries and keep follow-ups and payment confirmations visible to the team.
What I did
I used n8n and Make to connect enquiry capture, WhatsApp follow-up, payment confirmation and team notifications.
What changed
The client reported capturing about seven in ten daily leads after the workflow went live.
