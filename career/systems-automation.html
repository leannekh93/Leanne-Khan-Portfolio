Selected projects

Capturing pest-control enquiries during call-outs

The problem
The owner was paying to market his pest-control business, but he often missed calls while working on-site. Prospects needed a quick response, and the business wanted to keep using WhatsApp.
What I built
I added a chatbot to the Google Business booking flow that engaged prospects as soon as they arrived and asked whether they needed immediate help. I connected the flow to WhatsApp so the owner received the enquiry details and a prompt to follow up on the same channel he already used.
The result
The owner reported that lead capture rose from about 1 in 10 enquiries to about 7 in 10. He tracked enquiries daily for the first seven days after launch.

Getting legal documents from field phones into shared case records

The problem
A grassroots legal-services team needed to collect documents for clients in communities with limited access to legal services. Most field paralegals worked from their phones, while three office staff needed central access to case records.
What I built
I kept WhatsApp as the submission channel for five field workers and connected those submissions to a CRM and case-tracking dashboard I built for the office team. Field staff could submit documents from their phones, and office staff could monitor the case records centrally.
The result
The client reported saving money on CRM costs. The workflow gave office staff access to field submissions in one place, and the client later took the project to a developer for further work.
