Selected projects

Capturing pest-control enquiries during call-outs

The problem
The owner was paying to market his pest-control business, but he often missed calls while working on-site. Some customers had left negative reviews about his availability. Prospects needed a quick response, and the business wanted to keep using WhatsApp.
What I built
I ran a marketing campaign and added a chatbot to the Google Business booking flow and connected it to the field agent’s calendar, CRM and WhatsApp.

Immediate help: The bot asked what kind of pest or animal was involved. It routed the prospect to the field agent’s WhatsApp, alerted him with the enquiry details and used his calendar to book the next open slot. If his day was fully booked, snake removals were routed to a pest-control partner he had selected.

Non-urgent enquiries: The bot asked the prospect to describe what they needed, updated the CRM, added the enquiry to the follow-up list and directed the prospect to WhatsApp. Prospects could also opt in to receive updates about specials.

The result
The owner reported that lead capture rose from about 1 in 10 enquiries to about 7 in 10. He tracked enquiries daily for the first seven days after launch. The business received positive reviews, which lifted the owner’s morale. With the campaign and faster responses, he also extended his services to another community.

Getting legal documents from field phones into shared case records

The problem
A grassroots legal-services team needed to collect documents for clients in communities with limited access to legal services. Most field paralegals worked from their phones, while three office staff needed central access to case records.
What I built
I built a case-tracking system around this legal team’s own workflow. I kept WhatsApp as the submission channel for five field workers and connected it to a CRM and dashboard for the office team.

Existing case: A code number on the WhatsApp submission matched the document to the right case record.

New case: Field staff marked the submission NE for new entry. The workflow created a new reference number and case code so future documents could be matched to it.

The result
The client reported saving money on CRM costs. Office staff could view field submissions in one place. I signed over the system to the client after payment. Another group of paralegals later expressed interest in using the same system.
